Spin the globe. Light up a city.

Search any of more than 58,000 cities, tap, and it glows. Countries fill in with the color of their continent, and the years slider replays how your world grew.

A stamp for every country.

Every place you log earns a hand-inked passport stamp with its real flag and the day you arrived. Fill the pages, collect badges, chase the next goal.

A journal that does the counting.

See how much of the world you have explored, which continents you have covered and how far you have traveled. Keep notes on every trip.
