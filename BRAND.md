# Trip Track brand

**Tagline:** Light up your world. **Promise:** Track every trip, and watch your world light up one city at a time.
Live guide: `npm run dev` then open `/brand.html`. Logo sources are generated by `scripts/build-brand.py` into `public/brand/`.

## Personality
Curious, warm, proud without bragging, clear. A well-traveled friend, not an airline or an accountant.

## Logo
A pin riding an orbit. The pin is where you have been, the orbit is the track that got you there, the sun-colored traveler is the next trip. The dot of the "i" in the wordmark is the same lit city.
Files: `logo-horizontal(.svg|-dark.svg)`, `logo-stacked.svg`, `logo-mark.svg` (app icon), `logo-glyph.svg` (no tile), `favicon.svg`, `wordmark(.svg|-dark.svg)`.
Clear space: one pin-hole diameter. Minimum: icon 16 px, horizontal logo 96 px wide. Never stretch, recolor the tile, outline, or rotate it.

## Color (roughly 60% paper/night, 20% ink, 12% sky and tints, 8% coral)
Track Coral #FF5A2C (buttons use #D63F12 for contrast; dark mode #FF8A5C), Midnight Ink #12263F, Lagoon Sky #CDE7F7, Sunbeam #FFD35C, Lagoon Teal #1FB5A8, Paper #F1F7FC, Night #0A1424.

## Type
Bricolage Grotesque (headlines, numbers, wordmark; 700/800) and Figtree (everything else; tabular numerals for dates and distances).

## Voice
Second person, plain words, short sentences. "You've lit 12 cities." Not "Achievement unlocked: 12 city nodes!". Errors name the problem and the way out.

## Motifs
The Track (dashed arc with a traveler), the Glow (pulsing coral pin), the Stamp (passport stamps per country).
