Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Draft. This document describes how GlobeFolio works. It is in English only and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It must be reviewed, and the operator details below completed, before the accounts feature is opened to the public.

In short
  • GlobeFolio works without an account. In that mode your trips stay on your device and we never see them.
  • If you create an account, we store your trips in a private cloud database so they follow you across devices.
  • Everything is private by default. Friends or a public link see only what you choose, and never your notes.
  • We do not use your phone's location, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers. We never sell your data.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account at any time, inside the app.

3. Why we may process it (legal bases)

To provide the service you asked for, that is, the contract (account, sync, friends, shared trips); our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse; compliance with legal obligations; and your consent where we ask for it (for example, to make your map visible to friends or publicly, which you can change at any time).

4. Who can see your data

  • Only me (default): nobody else.
  • My friends: people whose friend request you accepted see the places and dates of your trips, except trips you hid. They never see your notes. Blocking a person removes them and hides everything from them.
  • Anyone with my link: a public page shows your handle, display name, places and dates, except hidden trips and never notes.
  • Shared trips: members you invite see the stops of that trip, including any notes added to those stops. Owners and editors can change them.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and real-time updates. We choose an EU region for storage.
  • Google and Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your email address and a stable account identifier. When you open the sign-in screen, your browser loads Google's sign-in button from Google, so Google learns that you opened it; nothing is loaded from Google anywhere else in the app.
  • An email delivery provider [to be named]: sends sign-in links.
  • GitHub Pages (or another static host we may move to): serves the app files. It sees technical request data such as your IP address.

These providers act on our instructions under data processing terms. Some may process data outside your country; where required we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you delete a trip it is marked deleted for up to 30 days so your other devices can learn about it, then removed. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live database immediately; copies in provider backups expire within [30] days. Technical logs are kept for [30] days or less.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, or California law) you can ask to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the use of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of this is built into the app: Journal, then Your account lets you download your data and delete your account. For anything else write to %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL%; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

California: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

8. Children

GlobeFolio is not for children under 13 (or the higher age required by your country). You must be at least 13 to create an account. If we learn that a younger child has an account, we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to every table is restricted by row level security rules so that each person can only reach their own rows, plus what others have chosen to share with them. No secret keys are kept in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Cookies and similar storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The app stores on your device: your trips (if you are not signed in, or as an offline copy if you are), your language and theme choice, and, when signed in, a sign-in token. These are strictly necessary for the app to work, so no consent banner is shown.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
