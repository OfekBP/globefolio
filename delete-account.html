Delete your GlobeFolio account

How to delete your account and the data stored in it.

In short
  • You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, in under a minute.
  • Deleting it permanently removes your profile, trips, wishes, friendships and shared-trip memberships from our servers.
  • GlobeFolio also works without an account. In that mode nothing is stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete there.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open GlobeFolio and tap the settings icon (top right of the map).
  2. Tap your account name, then Delete my account.
  3. Type your handle to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, tap Download my data in the same place.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (handle, display name, visibility setting, home city).
  • Every trip and wish stored in your account, including notes.
  • Your friendships and friend requests, and your membership of shared trips.
  • Your sign-in record. Backups that still hold it are overwritten within 30 days.

The trips saved on your own phone are not touched; they stay in the app as guest data until you erase them in Settings, Erase all my trips.

Cannot open the app?

Write to %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL% from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete the account and confirm within 30 days.
