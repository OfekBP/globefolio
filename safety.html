Safety and Community Standards

Last updated 12 November 2026

Who GlobeFolio is for

GlobeFolio is a personal travel map for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children. Your map is private by default. Nothing is visible to anyone else until you choose to share it with friends or a shared trip.

What is not allowed

Content or behavior that is hateful, harassing, threatening, sexual involving minors, that exposes someone's private information, or that impersonates another person. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) material or for any attempt to contact or exploit minors. Accounts involved are removed immediately and, where the law requires, reported to the authorities.

Your controls

  • Visibility: choose private, friends or public in Your account, and hide single trips from friends.
  • Block: blocking a person removes the friendship and stops all contact and sharing between you.
  • Report: open a person's profile in the Friends tab and tap Report, with a short reason. You can also write to the address below.
  • Delete: you can delete your account and all your data at any time (Journal, then Your account), or at the account deletion page.

What we do with reports

Every report is read by the operator. We aim to review reports within 72 hours, and sooner when someone may be in danger. Depending on what we find we may remove content, warn the person, suspend or delete the account. We do not tell the reported person who reported them. Reports are kept only as long as needed to protect the community and to comply with the law.

Profile pictures and names

Your picture, display name, bio and color are visible to people who can find you. Do not use pictures of other people without their permission, nudity, violence, hate symbols or anything that is not suitable for a 13+ audience. Use Report on a person's page to tell us about a picture, name or bio; we remove anything that breaks these rules and may remove the account.
