Tripglobe is a friendly home for everywhere you have been. This is how it looks, sounds and behaves, so every screen feels like the same warm, curious friend.

The idea

Purpose. Make every trip feel worth remembering, and every map a little more yours.

Promise. Track every trip, and watch your world fill in one city at a time.

Personality. Curious, warm, proud without bragging, clear. Think well-traveled friend, not airline or accountant.

Logo

A pin riding an orbit. The pin is where you have been, the orbit is the track that got you there, and the sunshine traveler is the next trip. The dot of the "i" is the same lit city.

Clear space. Keep one pin-hole diameter free on every side (about a quarter of the icon's height).

Minimum size. Icon 16 px. Horizontal logo 96 px wide. Below that, use the icon alone.

Color

A sunny, colorful atlas: every continent has its own pastel family on the globe, with Lagoon for actions and Sunshine for the places you have lit. Cream and Dusk Ink keep it grown-up.
