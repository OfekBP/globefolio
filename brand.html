Trip Track is a friendly home for everywhere you have been. This is how it looks, sounds and behaves, so every screen feels like the same warm, curious friend.

The idea

Purpose. Make every trip feel worth remembering, and every map a little more yours.

Promise. Track every trip, and watch your world fill in one city at a time.

Personality. Curious, warm, proud without bragging, clear. Think well-traveled friend, not airline or accountant.

Logo

A pin riding an orbit. The pin is where you have been, the orbit is the track that got you there, and the lime traveler is the next trip. The dot of the "i" is the same lit city.

Clear space. Keep one pin-hole diameter free on every side (about a quarter of the icon's height).

Minimum size. Icon 16 px. Horizontal logo 96 px wide. Below that, use the icon alone.

Don't. Stretch it, recolor the tile, outline it, rotate it, or set it on a busy photo. On photos, use the icon on a plain tile.

Color

Warm paper and dark ink, with pine green for places you have been and a highlighter lime used sparingly. Roughly 65% paper or night, 20% ink, 10% pine, 5% marker.

Type

DM Serif Display

Headlines, numbers and the wordmark. An old-style atlas serif with real contrast, one weight.

DM Sans

Everything you read: labels, notes, buttons. Plain and legible. Weights 400 to 700, tabular numerals for dates and distances.
